Research interests

My research interests center on data-driven modeling of infrastructure resilience and socio-technical systems under climate and hazard stressors. I am especially interested in how social infrastructure shapes community vulnerability across space. I am also interested in using spatial and statistical methods to develop practical decision-support tools for more resilient communities.

Social Infrastructure

Jan 2025 — May 2025

I studied how the density, diversity, and spatial distribution of social infrastructure shape community vulnerability to natural hazards across 25 major U.S. cities.

I integrated social infrastructure, hazard-event, socioeconomic, and geographic datasets and conducted statistical analyses in R. The analysis found that stronger social infrastructure was associated with lower property damage, injuries, and fatalities.

This project most directly shaped the research interests I pursue today.

AI Emissions Reporter

Aug 2024 — Dec 2024

I built an AI-powered report-generation pipeline using R, Python, and the OpenAI API to automate customized emissions reports for county-level policymakers and transportation emissions stakeholders.

The pipeline processed and decoded transportation emissions data across user-defined parameters such as geography, year, pollutant, vehicle type, and fuel type, producing tailored reports for climate-policy analysis.
